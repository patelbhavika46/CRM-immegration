Lead Management

Lead Management is the starting point of the sales process. This module is designed to capture, track, and manage potential customers (leads) until they are qualified. The flow covers viewing all leads in a filterable list, adding or editing lead details through a form, viewing a comprehensive 360-degree profile of a single lead, and finally, converting a qualified lead into an account, contact, and potential opportunity.

This module focuses on maintaining detailed personal records, immigration-specific details (e.g., visa type, application ID), and communication history for each individual involved in a case. Effective applicant management ensures all necessary personal information is tracked for successful case processing.

Account Management

For an immigration service business, 'Accounts' represent the primary client entities—whether individuals, families, or corporate clients—for whom immigration services are being provided. This module serves as the central hub for managing all case-related information, associated family members or dependents, service history, and critical documents. A well-structured account record provides a comprehensive view of each client's immigration journey and service needs.

This is the heart of the sales process, where potential deals are tracked and managed through the sales pipeline. This module allows sales teams to visualize their pipeline (often with a Kanban-style board), manage the stages of each deal, forecast revenue, and focus their efforts on closing sales. The goal is to provide clarity and control over the entire sales cycle.

Activity Management

In an immigration service business, 'Activities' are the crucial interactions and tasks that drive each client's case forward. This module helps consultants and case managers organize their daily work, track all communications (calls, emails, meetings), manage document requests, and schedule government appointments. It ensures that every step in the immigration process is meticulously recorded and followed up on, leading to efficient case progression and client satisfaction.

Integrations

For an immigration service business, the 'Integrations' module is vital for connecting the CRM with external systems that streamline operations and enhance client service. This includes direct links to government immigration portals for application submissions, payment gateways for secure transactions, and document management solutions for efficient storage and retrieval of sensitive client files. Seamless integrations reduce manual effort, minimize errors, and accelerate the immigration process.

System-Wide Elements

This section details UI components and interactions that are consistently available throughout the entire application. These global elements, such as search, notifications, and modals, ensure a predictable and efficient user experience. They provide users with essential tools and feedback mechanisms regardless of which module they are currently working in, unifying the application's design and functionality. For an immigration service CRM, these elements are tailored to quickly access client information, receive case updates, and manage critical actions.
